Welcome to Rapid Reader.

This device shows books one word at a time, a technique called Rapid
Serial Visual Presentation. Because your eyes never move, you can read
much faster than on paper. The orange letter with the small marks above
and below it is the Optimal Recognition Point. Keep your gaze fixed
there and let the words come to you.

The two small screens are peripheral by design. While you read, the
left one tracks your progress and the time remaining, and the right
one shows your speed. They are dimmed so they stay out of the way.

Right now you are reading in RSVP mode. The two keys are on the right
edge of the device: K1 is the upper key and K2 the lower one. Press K1
once to pause. Pausing shows the whole sentence, so you never lose your
place, and the right screen reminds you what each key does. Press K1
again to resume.

Press K2 once to jump back one sentence. Double-tap K2 to slow down.
Double-tap K1 to speed up. Triple-tap K1 to skip ahead one sentence.
Hold K2 for two seconds to save your spot and return to the library.

In the library, tap K2 to move down and double-tap K2 to move up. Tap
K1 to open the selected book. Your position in every book is saved
automatically, so you can switch between books freely.

To add books, connect to this device over your network and copy plain
text or epub files into the ebooks folder. The address is shown in the
README that came with this card.

When you are done reading, hold K2 in the library and confirm with K1
to power down safely. Wait until all three screens go dark before
unplugging.

Start slow. Two hundred fifty words per minute feels brisk at first.
As your brain adapts, nudge the speed up. Comprehension usually holds
surprisingly well up to double your normal reading speed.

Happy reading.
