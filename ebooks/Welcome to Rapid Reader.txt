Welcome to Rapid Reader.

This device shows books one word at a time, a technique called Rapid
Serial Visual Presentation. Because your eyes never move, you can read
much faster than on paper. The bold letter with the small marks above
and below it is the Optimal Recognition Point. Keep your gaze fixed
there and let the words come to you.

Right now you are reading in RSVP mode. Press button six once to
pause. Pausing shows the whole sentence, so you never lose your place.
Press six again to resume.

Press button five once to jump back one sentence. Double-tap five to
slow down. Double-tap six to speed up. Triple-tap six to skip ahead
one sentence. Hold five for two seconds to save your spot and return
to the library.

In the library, tap five to move down and double-tap five to move up.
Tap six to open the selected book. Your position in every book is
saved automatically, so you can switch between books freely.

To add books, connect to this device over your network and copy plain
text or epub files into the ebooks folder. The address is shown in the
manual that came with this card.

When you are done reading, hold five in the library and confirm with
six to power down safely. The screen keeps its last image even with
the power off; that is normal for electronic ink.

Start slow. One hundred fifty words per minute feels brisk at first.
As your brain adapts, nudge the speed up. Comprehension usually holds
surprisingly well up to double your normal reading speed.

Happy reading.
